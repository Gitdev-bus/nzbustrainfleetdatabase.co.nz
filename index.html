ABOUT US

Created by a New Zealand Transit Enthusiast

New Zealand Bus & Train Fleet Database (NZBTFD) was created by Christian Yeung, a 13-year-old public transport enthusiast from Auckland.

I started this website because I enjoy buses, trains and finding out more about the vehicles operating around New Zealand. I wanted to put the information I have collected into one place so other people can easily look through it.

The database includes fleet numbers, vehicle types, operators, destination displays, liveries, refurbishments, loans and demonstrator vehicles.

My interest in public transport has also been featured in the media. In June 2026, The Times published an article titled "Young bus enthusiast has solutions for Auckland's network", which covered some of my ideas for Auckland's bus network, including route planning and service frequency.

In August 2026, RNZ also featured me in an article about the new City Rail Link station at Maungawhau. You can read the RNZ article here. It mentioned my visit to the station and my interest in using the train during the school holidays.

I will continue adding new records and updating information as I find out more about New Zealand's bus and train fleets.
