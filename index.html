ABOUT US

Created by a New Zealand transit enthusiast

New Zealand Bus & Train Fleet Database (NZBTFD) was created by Christian Yeung, a 13-year-old public transport enthusiast from Auckland, New Zealand. With a strong interest in buses, trains and fleet research, Christian created the website to bring information about New Zealand's public transport fleets together in one place. The database covers fleet numbers, vehicle types, operators, destination displays, liveries, interior refurbishments, loans and demonstrators.

Christian's interest in public transport has also been featured in the media. In June 2026, The Times published an article titled “Young bus enthusiast has solutions for Auckland's network”, highlighting his ideas for improving Auckland's bus network, including route planning and service frequency.

In August 2026, Christian was also featured in RNZ's coverage of Aucklanders getting their first look inside the new City Rail Link station at Maungawhau. The article mentioned his visit to Maungawhau Station and his excitement about using the train during the school holidays and having better connections to western Auckland.

Through the NZBTFD, Christian aims to make New Zealand public transport fleet information easier to find, explore and understand.
